          Rules and payouts

          How funded accounts work

          What counts as a trade?

          Each sell is one closed trade. A sell is a win when you get back more SOL than that portion cost you, after fees.

          What ends a challenge?

          If your equity (free SOL plus open positions) falls below the max-loss line even once, the challenge fails. You can restart any time.

          How are payouts made?

          Funded traders are paid their profit split in SOL to the wallet they applied with, every 14 days.

          Can I lose my own money?

          No. You never deposit. If a funded account hits its loss limit, it closes and you can take a new challenge.

          Memecoin basics

          Read this before your first ape

          Short definitions of the terms you'll see on pump.fun and Solana DEXs, with the numbers worked out.

          Market cap

          Price times total supply. Every pump.fun coin has 1 billion tokens, so a $0.00003 price means a $30K market cap.

          0.00003 × 1,000,000,000 = $30,000

          Bonding curve

          A new pump.fun coin trades against an automatic curve. The price rises as people buy and falls as they sell, until the curve sells out.

          Starts with ~30 virtual SOL · ~793M tokens for sale

          Graduating / migrating

          When the bonding curve sells out, the coin "graduates" and its liquidity moves to a regular pool on PumpSwap. These show up in the Migrated tab.

          Bonding 100% → migrated to a PumpSwap pool

          Price impact

          How far your own order pushes the price. On a curve holding 30 SOL, buying 3 SOL moves it about 10%. You pay it on the way in and again on the way out.

          impact ≈ your SOL ÷ SOL in the pool

          Slippage limit

          The maximum price change you'll accept before an order executes. Too low and orders fail on thin coins. Too high and bots can front-run you.

          1% majors · 5–10% migrated memes · 10–25% fresh launches

          Rug pull

          A creator or large holder dumps their tokens and the price collapses. Most brand-new launches never recover.

          Watch a coin in the New tab for 10 minutes

          Position sizing

          How much of your account goes into one trade. Professionals risk a small slice so a rug can't end them.

          100 SOL account · 1–3 SOL per new-launch trade

          Paper hands / diamond hands

          Selling at the first dip versus holding through everything. Neither is a strategy. Decide your exit before you enter.

          Plan: take profit at +40%, cut at −20%
          Before you buy a real token

          Safety checklist

          Check these on a block explorer or a token scanner before you put real SOL into a new memecoin.

          • Mint authority revokedOtherwise the creator can print more tokens and dilute you.
          • Freeze authority revokedOtherwise your tokens can be frozen so you can't sell.
          • Liquidity burned or lockedUnlocked liquidity can be pulled in one transaction.
          • Top 10 holders under ~30%Concentrated supply means a few wallets can dump on everyone.
          • Real volume, real communityWash-traded volume and bot-filled chats are common. Look at holder growth over hours, not minutes.
